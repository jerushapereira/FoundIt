PROTOTYPE INFORMATION

Terms and conditions

These terms describe the intended use of the current FoundIt classroom prototype. It is a demonstration interface, not an operational lost-and-found service.

Demonstration only

The registry includes sample entries for demonstrating search and item actions. Entries you create exist only in the current page's memory and disappear when you refresh or close the page.

No service or recovery guarantee

The prototype is not monitored by campus staff and does not connect to the C program. Do not rely on it to report an actual loss, arrange a handover, or confirm ownership of an item.

Appropriate use

For demonstration, enter only fictional or non-sensitive item details. Do not include personal contact details, confidential information, or information that could put someone at risk.

Future deployment

Before using a production service, its owner should provide applicable institutional rules, moderation and handover procedures, support contacts, and terms that reflect the deployed system.
